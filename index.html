01.

The way I think

I enjoy challenging things because they make me think critically, test my understanding and discover what I am capable of. When something is difficult, I keep working until I understand it.

I'm currently studying Business Intelligence and Data Analytics at Botswana Accountancy College. My interest lies in understanding how information can help organisations see patterns, solve problems and make informed decisions.

I'm especially interested in the banking industry. Banks work with information about customers, services and business performance every day. I want to learn how analysts turn that information into useful insights that help banks serve people better.

Outside my studies, I enjoy playing netball, listening to music and writing. Writing is my quiet place. It gives me room to organise my thoughts, express myself and make sense of the world around me.

Where I'm headed

Turning curiosity into a career.

My goal is to become a trusted and respected Data Analyst in the banking industry. I want to contribute to evidence-based decisions that improve services and create better experiences for customers.

In the long term, I hope to grow into a Senior Business Intelligence Analyst. Just as importantly, I want to become a woman whose journey encourages younger girls to believe that they, too, can build successful careers in data and technology.
