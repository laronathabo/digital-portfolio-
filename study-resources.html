My study habits

How I want to learn

I learn best when I combine understanding with practice. Rather than simply reading notes, I want to give myself opportunities to apply each new concept and reflect on what I still need to improve.

1

Set one clear objective

Decide what I want to understand or be able to do before beginning a study session.

2

Practise with examples

Use small datasets and exercises to apply concepts rather than relying on memorisation alone.

3

Explain what I learned

Write a short summary in my own words and explain why a formula, query or visualisation works.

4

Review mistakes

Treat errors as useful feedback. Identify what went wrong, correct it and try the task again.

5

Keep a record of progress

Save useful notes, exercises and practice projects so I can see how my skills develop over time.
