Page 07 · Professional Communication

A good opportunity can begin with one email.

Professional communication is part of presenting myself as a developing Business Intelligence and Data Analytics student. This email template demonstrates how I would introduce myself to a bank and enquire about possible industrial attachment opportunities.

Email composition · Internship enquiry

New message

From thabolarona1@icloud.com
To firstnationalbank@gmail.com
Subject Enquiry About Industrial Attachment – Data Analyst Intern

Dear Recruitment Team,

I hope this email finds you well.

My name is Larona Thabo, and I am a first-year student at Botswana Accountancy College, pursuing Business Intelligence and Data Analytics. I am writing to enquire about the possibility of undertaking an industrial attachment within your organisation, particularly in a data analysis or business intelligence-related role.

I am interested in understanding how banks use data to identify customer needs, monitor performance and support informed business decisions. Through my studies, I am developing foundational skills in Microsoft Excel, basic SQL, reporting and data presentation. I am eager to strengthen these skills through practical experience and learn from professionals in the field.

I would appreciate it if you could advise me whether any suitable industrial attachment opportunities are available, or whether there is a formal application process I should follow.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I would be grateful for any guidance you can provide.

Kind regards,

Larona Thabo
Business Intelligence and Data Analytics Student
Botswana Accountancy College
thabolarona1@icloud.com

01

Keep it clear.

A professional email should explain who you are, why you are contacting the organisation and what response you are hoping for.

02

Before you send

  • Replace the recipient placeholder with a verified address.
  • Check the bank's correct recruitment or attachment process.
  • Use a clear, relevant subject line.
  • Attach your CV if requested or appropriate.
  • Proofread the message and confirm your contact details.
03

My communication goal

I want my communication to reflect professionalism, respect and genuine willingness to learn. Even when I am asking about an opportunity rather than applying for an advertised position, I can make my purpose clear and communicate confidently.
