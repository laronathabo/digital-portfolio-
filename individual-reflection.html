Growing into
the person I'm becoming.

Learning is not always about getting everything right the first time. Sometimes it is about recognising what I do not know, being willing to ask questions and finding the courage to keep going.

My learning journey / 2026

“I don't have to know everything today. I just have to be willing to learn.”

A reminder to myself

Before I begin

I am learning to appreciate progress, even when it happens slowly and quietly.

01

Starting with curiosity My experience as a first-year student

Beginning my studies in Business Intelligence and Data Analytics at Botswana Accountancy College has given me an opportunity to explore an area that connects with the way I like to think. I have always enjoyed challenges because they make me stop, consider different possibilities and work towards understanding something properly.

As I learn more about data and its role in business, I am beginning to appreciate that information is valuable when people know how to interpret it and use it responsibly. I am particularly interested in the banking industry because banks need to understand their customers, recognise patterns and make decisions that support better services.

I know that I am still at the beginning of my professional journey. Rather than expecting myself to have all the answers, I want to approach my studies with an open mind, a willingness to practise and a determination to improve.

My starting point is not a limitation. It is an opportunity to build a strong foundation.
02

Learning through difficulty Patience, effort and critical thinking

One thing I understand about myself is that I do not enjoy giving up simply because something is difficult. I would rather spend time trying to understand it than pretend I know something when I do not. This attitude is important in a field where information must be examined carefully and conclusions should be supported by evidence.

Studying a subject that involves data and analytical thinking also reminds me that being hardworking is not only about spending more time on a task. It is about learning to approach problems logically, checking my understanding and recognising when I need to find another explanation or ask for help.

I want to continue developing the confidence to ask questions, learn from mistakes and accept constructive feedback. I see these habits as part of becoming an independent learner rather than signs of weakness.

03

Discovering my strengths What I bring and what I want to improve

My experience as Deputy Head Girl at Saint Kizito Catholic School in 2025 helped me appreciate the importance of responsibility, communication and respectful leadership. Supporting the Head Girl and representing fellow students taught me that leadership involves listening to people and taking responsibilities seriously.

I can also draw on my interests outside the classroom. Writing gives me an opportunity to organise my thoughts and express ideas clearly, while netball reminds me of the value of cooperation and contributing to a shared effort.

As I continue my studies, I want to strengthen my technical abilities in Excel and SQL, become more confident in presenting information, and develop a better understanding of data visualisation. These are areas I can keep practising as I work towards my career goals.

04

Becoming the professional I imagine My ambitions and next steps

My long-term goal is to become a respected Data Analyst in the banking industry and eventually grow into a Senior Business Intelligence Analyst. I want to contribute to decisions that help banks understand their customers and improve the services they provide.

I recognise that achieving this goal will require more than ambition. I need to strengthen my technical skills, complete my studies, learn from practical experience and remain open to feedback. Seeking an industrial attachment will be an important opportunity to understand how analytical work happens in a real business environment.

I also hope that my future career will allow me to encourage younger girls who are interested in technology and analytics. I want them to see that they can be curious, creative, ambitious and capable of contributing meaningfully in fields that rely on data.

For now, my responsibility is to make the most of the learning opportunities available to me. I want to leave this stage of my education with stronger skills, greater confidence and a clearer understanding of the kind of professional I want to become.

My takeaway

Growth is something I practise, not something I wait for.

This stage of my journey is teaching me to take my ambitions seriously while giving myself room to learn. I may still have a great deal to discover, but I can keep moving forward through curiosity, effort and a willingness to improve.
